Street to Shelf.

How one photograph becomes two products.

01

Shot on the street

No staging, no studio — every piece starts as a photograph taken around the city.

02

Reworked into art

The photograph is rebuilt as a standalone composition, framed and sold as a print.

03

Printed onto the garment

The finished artwork — not the raw photo — is what ends up on the tee, so the print on your wall and the one on your chest are the same piece.
